Cozy Driver

Click the toolbar icon, or press Alt+D, to open the game beside whatever you are reading or watching.

What this extension can see
Nothing. A default install has no host permissions and no content script, so it cannot read a single page you visit. It stores two settings and makes no network requests of its own. The game is bundled rather than downloaded, which is why it also works with no connection.
Controls
W/S throttle and brake · A/D steer · Space handbrake · Shift fine control · R back to the road · C camera · V next car · N radio · J music window · Esc garage. A gamepad works too.
